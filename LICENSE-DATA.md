# Licence of the data and results

The source code (`src/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`) is released under the MIT License (`LICENSE`).

The simulation results (`results/`), the configuration files (`configs/`) and the processed operating records
(`DATA/processed/`) are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please cite the article and this project when you reuse them.

Third-party data:

- `DATA/raw/nasa_power/`: hourly irradiance from the NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project
  (https://power.larc.nasa.gov/), which is freely available; please acknowledge the POWER Project when you reuse it.
- `DATA/processed/`: processed operating records of the Kegeyli irrigation system (Republic of Karakalpakstan,
  Uzbekistan), transcribed by the authors from the daily operating records and the 2021 vegetation plan
  (`DATA/processed/README.md`).
