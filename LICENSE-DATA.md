# Licence of the data and results

The source code (`src/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `app/`) is released under the MIT License (`LICENSE`).

The simulation results (`results/`), the configuration files (`configs/`), the hypothesis protocol (`requirements/`),
and the synthetic inputs (`DATA/synthetic/`) are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please cite the article and this repository when
you reuse them (`CITATION.cff`).

Third-party data:

- `DATA/raw/nasa_power/`: hourly irradiance from the NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project
  (https://power.larc.nasa.gov/), which is freely available; please acknowledge the POWER Project when you reuse it.
- The processed operating records of the Kegeyli irrigation system (`DATA/processed/`) are not part of this
  repository. They are third-party data and are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.
